Samples of formats Zipper refuses, for tests/refused.rs.

lzx.cab is cab-crate-lzx-two-files.cab from tests/data/cab-corpus before
12 November 2026: the byte string of the read_lzx_cabinet_with_two_files
test in src/cabinet.rs of the cab crate 0.6.0,
https://github.com/mdsteele/rust-cab, used under the MIT License that
tests/data/cab-corpus/NOTICE.txt reproduces, Copyright (c) 2017 Matthew D.
Steele.

lzx.wim.xz and lzms.wim.xz are lzx-128k.wim and lzms-one-chunk.wim from
tests/data/wim-corpus before 12 November 2026, compressed with xz. Both
were written by wimlib-imagex 1.14.5 from trees of seeded pseudo-random
data that tests/wim_corpus.rs generated. They hold no third-party content.

chm.chm.xz is small.chm from tests/data/chm-corpus before 12 November
2026, compressed with xz. chmcmd from Free Pascal 3.2.2 wrote it from
generated data. It holds no third-party content.

compound.doc.xz and installer.msi.xz are generated-v4-storages.doc and
generated-v3-two-cabs.msi from tests/data/msi-corpus before 12 November
2026, compressed with xz. Zipper's own compound file test writer wrote
them. They hold no third-party content.

The disk image samples come from tests/data/disk-corpus before
12 November 2026. They were written on macOS from seeded pseudo-random
data that tests/disk_corpus.rs generated, and hold no third-party content.
mkfs.fat 4.2 formatted the FAT volumes inside them and mtools 4.0.50
filled them.

- dynamic.vhd.xz and raw-fat.vhd.xz are VHD images and blocks-1m.vhdx.xz
  a VHDX image, written by qemu-img 11.1.3.
- fat12.qcow.xz, mbr-one.qcow2.xz, fat12-static.vdi.xz and
  mbr-one.vmdk.xz were converted from raw disks by qemu-img 11.1.3.
  gpt-two-flat.vmdk.xz is the descriptor of a monolithic flat VMDK that
  qemu-img wrote. Its extent is not kept, since only the descriptor's
  first line is checked.
- hfs-gpt.qcow2.xz and apfs-gpt.vdi.xz wrap GPT disks that hdiutil on
  macOS 26 wrote, holding HFS+ and APFS volumes.
- mbr-two.simg.xz is an Android sparse image from Zipper's former disk
  test writer.
- gpt-two.img.xz is a GPT disk that sgdisk 1.0.10 partitioned, and
  mbr-two.img.xz an MBR disk from Zipper's former disk test writer. Each
  is cut to its first 1 MiB, which holds the partition tables.
