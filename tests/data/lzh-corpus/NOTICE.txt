https://github.com/fragglet/lhasa, at commit 75ed83559f23e9538e0045c62f53f77ab03d03d6.

lhasa-lha_os2_208-h3_lh5.lzh is test/archives/lha_os2_208/h3_lh5.lzh. They are used

with LHa for UNIX 1.14i-ac built from https://github.com/jca02266/lha, from
