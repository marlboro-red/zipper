Every image in this folder was written on macOS through write_the_corpus in
tests/disk_corpus.rs, from trees of seeded pseudo-random data that the test
generates. mkfs.fat 4.2 formatted the FAT12, FAT16 and FAT32 volumes and
mtools 4.0.50 filled them. sgdisk 1.0.10 wrote the GPT tables, the test
writer in tests/common/disk_writer.rs wrote the MBR tables, and qemu-img
11.1.3 wrote the VHD and VHDX images. No file holds third-party content.

write_the_container_corpus added the QCOW, VDI, VMDK and Android sparse
images from the raw disks above. qemu-img 11.1.3 converted them to QCOW 1,
QCOW 2 with and without zlib clusters, VDI dynamic and static, and VMDK
monolithic sparse and monolithic flat, whose descriptor and extent are
gpt-two-flat.vmdk and gpt-two-flat-flat.vmdk. The test writer wrote
gpt-one-stream.vmdk, a stream-optimized VMDK in the layout VMware writes,
since 7-Zip 26.05 cannot open the layout qemu-img writes, and mbr-two.simg.
hdiutil on macOS 26 wrote the GPT disks holding HFS+ and APFS volumes inside
hfs-gpt.qcow2 and apfs-gpt.vdi. ntfs-mbr.vmdk wraps ntfs3g-basic.ntfs from
tests/data/ntfs-corpus in an MBR from the test writer.

trees.json records the tree 7zz 26.05 extracted from each one.
